✦ FAQ

Common questions.

How is this different from your affiliate program?

The affiliate program is open to anyone and runs through self-service signup. It starts at 20% for 12 months per referral and climbs to 30% for 24 months once you pass 200 sales, with payouts 90 days after month-end. The Ambassador Program is application-based, pays 30% from your first referral for the lifetime of the account, pays every 30 days, and includes intel, beta access, team access, content collabs, and program support you do not get as an affiliate.

What does "lifetime" actually mean?

You earn your 30% on a referred account for as long as that account keeps paying. There is no 12-month window and no step-down. If they upgrade or add on, your share of that account goes up with it.

When do I get paid?

Every 30 days. We keep the payout method flexible for ambassadors and set it up with you during onboarding.

How long does my referral link stay attached to someone?

90 days. If they click your link and sign up any time inside that window, the referral is yours.

Can I leave the program?

Anytime, with 30 days' notice. Your existing referrals keep paying out. Lifetime means lifetime, whether or not you are still active in the program.

Can I be removed from the program?

Yes, and it matters which kind. If you go inactive, you lose your place in the program at the annual review, but your existing commissions continue. If you are removed for cause, meaning black-hat tactics, misleading customers, or breaking the terms, commissions stop with the removal.

Can I be both an affiliate and an ambassador?

Yes. Commissions do not stack on the same referral.

Can I bring someone else into the program?

Once you have been active for a bit, yes, up to two sponsored applications per year. You vouch for them, they skip the review queue, and you earn a 5% override on their commissions for 12 months. The override is the one part of the program that is not lifetime.

How long does the review take?

Usually under a week from application to call. Sometimes faster.

What happens if I am not accepted?

We will tell you why. In most cases it is a fit issue, not a quality issue. The affiliate program may be the right home, and we will point you there.

Are commissions stackable with the discount on my own plan?

Yes. Your plan discount and your referral commissions are separate.

What counts as one piece of content per quarter?

Anything authentic in your native format. A YouTube video, a podcast mention, a newsletter feature, a tutorial post, a case study, a livestream demo. We are not counting words, we are looking for genuine work.

Do I need to use Vista Social to apply?

You need to be using it, or actively onboarding, by the time you are accepted. We do not accept partners who promote products they have not worked with.

Ready when you are.

The first 25 accepted become Founding Ambassadors, with permanent recognition in the program. After that the program stays open, but Founding status does not.
