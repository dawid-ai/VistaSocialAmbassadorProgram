FAQ

Common questions.

How is this different from your affiliate program?

The affiliate program is open to anyone, pays 20%, and runs through self-service signup. The Ambassador Program is application-based, pays 30% for 12 months, and includes intel, beta access, team access, content collabs, and program support you don't get as an affiliate.

Can I be both an affiliate and an ambassador?

Yes. But commissions don't stack on the same referral.

Can I bring someone else into the program?

Once you've been active for a bit, yes — up to two sponsored applications per year. You vouch for them, they skip the review queue, and you earn a 5% override on their commissions for 12 months.

How long does the review take?

Usually under a week from application to call. Sometimes faster.

What happens if I'm not accepted?

We'll tell you why. In most cases it's a fit issue, not a quality issue — the affiliate program may be the right home, and we'll point you there.

Can I leave the program?

Anytime, with 30 days' notice. Commissions on existing referrals continue through their 12-month window.

Can I be removed from the program?

Yes. Ambassadors who go inactive are removed at the annual review. We also remove anyone using black-hat tactics or misrepresenting the product. We'd rather have 50 active ambassadors than 500 dormant ones.

Are commissions stackable with the discount on my own plan?

Yes — your plan discount and your referral commissions are separate.

What counts as "one piece of content per quarter"?

Anything authentic in your native format. A YouTube video, a podcast mention, a newsletter feature, a tutorial post, a case study, a livestream demo. We're not counting words — we're looking for genuine work.

Do I need to use Vista Social to apply?

You need to be using it (or actively onboarding) by the time you're accepted. We don't accept partners who promote products they haven't worked with.

Ready when you are.

First 25 accepted become Founding Ambassadors — rates locked in for as long as you're active, plus permanent recognition in the program. After that, the program stays open, but Founding status doesn't.
