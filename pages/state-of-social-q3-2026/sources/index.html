Evidence key

  • A: official policy or filing, primary dataset, peer-reviewed experiment, or reproducible research.
  • B: strong observational study, transparent third-party measurement, or direct platform executive statement.
  • C: platform marketing metric, limited sample, trade report, practitioner test, or preprint awaiting review.
  • D: rumor, unauthenticated leak, or unsupported algorithm lore. Not used for a headline claim.

Platform performance figures receive a C when the underlying data and denominator are not independently auditable, even when the page itself is an official source. Official policy pages receive an A for what the rule says, not for whether the rule works as intended.
