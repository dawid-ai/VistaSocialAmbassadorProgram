03 / 16
The shift

Affiliate vs. Ambassador.

Standard affiliate

Self-service link program

  • 20% commission for 12 months
  • Self-service signup, no vetting
  • Generic landing page
  • No communication with the team
  • Relationship can't expand beyond link, tracking, payout
Ambassador

Curated partnership

  • 30% commission for 12 months — extendable to 18 or 24 for partnerships
  • Application + 30-min vetting call
  • Custom co-branded lander at vistasocial.com/a/{slug}
  • Beta access · team access · intel reports · content collabs
  • Filtered for content quality and tactic alignment

Fair exchange

What we ask.

Ambassadors who go quiet are removed at annual review. The shortlist stays short on purpose.

1

Proper, long-form content across all platforms. Planned, researched, well-created. Quarterly minimum.

2

FTC disclosure on anything promotional.

3

Annual review. Inactive ambassadors removed. Re-application allowed.

4

No black-hat plays — no brand bidding, fake reviews, coupon farms.

5

Be a real Vista Social user. No promoting tools you don't use.
